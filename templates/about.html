About

CyberLens AI is a university lab project from Manipal University Jaipur, built by Kuldeep Mishra and Samay Shakya. It is a desktop tool: one user, one machine, no accounts, no database. Uploaded files live in a temporary folder, are never modified, and are deleted when you ask or when an hour passes.

What it is not

It is not a firewall, a scanner, an intrusion prevention system or a penetration testing tool. It never connects to a network target and never executes content from an uploaded file. Uploaded files are treated as data, nothing more.

Where its opinions come from

The classifier was trained on a synthetic corpus generated by this project, not on captured real-world traffic. It is a demonstration of a complete analysis pipeline. It knows seven categories: normal traffic plus the six attack types on the home page. Everything it flags deserves a human read before anyone acts on it.

Trying it

The sample files in data/samples/ are good starting points: auth_bruteforce.log for a realistic authentication log, or clean_normal.csv to see the honest nothing-found state.
